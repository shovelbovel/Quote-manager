About QuoteManager

QuoteManager is a powerful service quote management system designed to streamline your business operations and help you win more clients.

Our Mission

We believe that small businesses and service providers should have access to powerful tools that simplify their operations and help them present a professional image to clients. Our mission is to make quote management simple, efficient, and accessible to businesses of all sizes.

With QuoteManager, you can create professional quotes in minutes, track their status, and maintain a complete history of all changes. Our platform helps you stay organized, respond quickly to client requests, and ultimately close more deals.

For Service Providers

  • Create professional quotes in minutes
  • Track quote status and client responses
  • Export quotes in PDF or Excel format
  • Share quotes directly with clients
  • Maintain a history of all modifications

For Administrators

  • Manage users and their permissions
  • Oversee all quotes across the organization
  • View comprehensive analytics and reports
  • Configure services and pricing templates
  • Monitor system activity and performance

For Clients

  • Receive professionally formatted quotes
  • View quotes online or as PDF attachments
  • Respond to quotes with a simple click
  • Ask questions and request modifications
  • Track the progress of accepted quotes
